🤔 Guess the Word by Emoji LIVE

A live quiz on YouTube, all day and all night: 🐝 + 🍯 = ? Guess the word from the emoji, right in the chat.

How to play

  1. Open the Guess the Word by Emoji LIVE stream on YouTube.
  2. An emoji puzzle appears with a category and a countdown. Type the answer in the live chat (small typos are fine).
  3. Get it right and your name pops up on screen with your points. The fastest answer gets 🥇 and the most points.
  4. The answer is revealed, and the next puzzle comes.
  • 💡 Halfway through, some letters of the answer appear.
  • 🔥 Every 5th puzzle is a HARD ONE worth double points.
  • 🔥 Answer puzzles in a row to build a streak.
  • ❤️ Every 10 likes give everyone DOUBLE POINTS for 5 puzzles.
  • ⭐ A new subscriber makes the next puzzle a GOLDEN PUZZLE worth triple points for everyone.
  • 🏆 The best players today and this week are shown on the leaderboard.
